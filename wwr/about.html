Rafting Splash

Rafting Splash is an adventure company founded by friends Lucas and Marina to share their love for the Emerald River. Known for exciting, safe rafting experiences and a strong commitment to protecting nature, the company attracts travelers from around the world. Every adventure begins with a splash and a passion for the river.

History

From its very first season, Rafting Splash became known for its vibrant energy, expert guides, and unforgettable experiences. Visitors from all over the world came to navigate the Emerald River’s winding rapids, each trip filled with laughter, adrenaline, and breathtaking scenery. The company’s motto — “Adventure begins with a single splash” — quickly became famous.

Over time, Rafting Splash expanded its offerings: night rafting with lanterns, multi-day river expeditions, and even “calm waters” programs for families and beginners. Yet no matter how much the company grew, one thing never changed — every journey down the river started with a warm welcome, a splash of water, and a promise of adventure.
